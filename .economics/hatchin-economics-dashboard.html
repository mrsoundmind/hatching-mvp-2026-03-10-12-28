Why chatting is nearly free

  • Simple messages run on Meta's open-source Llama 3.3-70B, about nothing per message (self-host or free tier).
  • Real conversation runs on DeepSeek Flash with the prompt cached (~98% cheaper), about $0.0004 (₹0.03) a message.
  • 1 million tokens ≈ 750,000 words ≈ 300 messages. $1 (₹86) covers ~2,000 messages.
  • One risk: DeepSeek is a single provider on promo pricing. Gemini and Llama stay as fallbacks.

The usage cap

LIVEa token budget that refills, like Claude

Background work draws from a budget that refills every 5 hours, with a weekly ceiling. Because one task can cost 8× another, the cap is a token budget, not a task count, so the worst case holds whatever the task mix. Below is that worst case per plan.

  • 1 million tokens ≈ 750,000 words ≈ 10 novels ≈ 300 messages.
  • $1 (₹86) of AI covers roughly 2,000 chat messages.

The pricing debate

ADVISORSstay at $19, or charge more?

A simulated panel of famous marketers argued $19 itself may be the trap: it puts you next to ChatGPT in people's minds, which caps what you can charge. Four of five wanted you to reframe and charge more. One argued the opposite.

Charge more, and reframe

Hormozi, Dunford, Sutherland, Halbert
  • Call it "your first 30 hires," not an "AI chat tool," so people compare it to a salary.
  • Test a higher price, $39 to $99 (₹3,354 to ₹8,514), with a results guarantee.
  • Sell to the people who already pay for AI in dollars. They exist in India too.

Do not chase everyone

Godin, the dissenter
  • Serving India, the US and companies at once is too much for one founder.
  • Pick the smallest group you can delight, maybe 1,000 founders who would pay $50 to $100 (₹4,300 to ₹8,600).
  • Be strict with the free plan. Giving everything to non-payers burns your dollars.

Simulated: built from published frameworks, not real opinions about Hatchin. Worth an A/B test before committing.
